How It Works

1

Install Locally

Nixite runs on your machine with full access to your NixOS system. No cloud services, no authentication, just direct control.

2

Browse Visually

Explore packages by category, purpose, or use voice commands. "I need to edit videos" finds all video editors for you.

3

Install with One Click

No terminal commands needed. Nixite handles all the complexity while you focus on what you want to accomplish.

4

Stay in Control

See exactly what will be installed. Preview configuration changes. Roll back if needed. It's your system, your way.
